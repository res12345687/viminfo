About VimInfo

We help organizations modernize, automate, and grow.

VimInfo is a digital transformation consultancy based in Delhi NCR. We work with SMEs, large enterprises, and government and PSU teams to turn technology into results they can measure. That means fewer manual processes, lower IT costs, better decisions from their data, and teams that actually use the new tools.

Many transformation projects stall between the strategy deck and the shop floor. We cover the whole journey, from deciding what to change to making sure it keeps working after go-live.

What we do

  • AI adoption: We find the AI use cases with the highest return in your business and run focused pilots, from copilots to workflow automation.
  • Cloud and IT modernization: We move legacy systems to the cloud and cut the cost of running them.
  • Process automation: We map manual, error-prone workflows across departments and vendors, then automate them.
  • Digital strategy: We build board-ready roadmaps that tie technology spending to business goals.
  • Government and PSU advisory: We run digital readiness assessments and support public-sector rollouts.
  • Data and analytics: We bring scattered data together into dashboards and reports people trust.

How we work

  1. Assess: We learn how your organization runs today and where the real gaps are.
  2. Design: We build a practical roadmap sized to your budget, timelines, and team.
  3. Implement: We deliver in stages, with clear milestones and measurable results.
  4. Sustain: We train your people and refine the systems so the gains last.
